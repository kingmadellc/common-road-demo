A MODERN SURVIVAL PILGRIMAGE

Somewhere the signal
doesn’t follow.

A rumored refuge in the middle of America. No cell phones. No computers. No digital devices. People have deliberately disconnected to own their homes, their work and their time.

Keep the worn frontier character of Last Free County. Give the name more mystery.

TITLE CONCEPT / QUIET COUNTRY

Leave the signal. Keep your life.

THE PLACE AT THE END OF THE ROAD

A new life.
A deliberate goodbye.

“They still have hot water. They just don’t need a password for it.”
— Sarah Mercer

The ban is a founding rule. Phones, computers and digital products stay outside the Line. Switching them off is not enough. An analog radio, a mechanical pump and a paper atlas can cross.

The promise is tangible. A house you own, work you choose, lights you switch on yourself. Entry is voluntary. Residents can leave. The rule applies to the people running the place, too.

The goodbye should matter. Ben’s dead handheld still holds the life he lost. A proposed border scene asks what the family can bring into this new one. The place can be worth reaching without every sacrifice being easy.
