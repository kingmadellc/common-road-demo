# Signals End

San Francisco, 2041. Nine mandatory apps control ordinary life. Private companies built the system; the government made it compulsory and handed more decisions to AI. One disputed employment report can close a household’s access to money, food and housing before anyone hears an appeal.

**[Play the demo](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/?v=0.8.6)** · **[Opening and departure](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/?v=0.8.6&departure=1&film=1)** · **[World and brand kit](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/brand/)**

A 116-second illustrated film introduces Jack’s exhaustion, Bea’s smuggled radio, Ben’s discovery, and three nights of family debate. A voice mentions an analog refuge in the Ozarks. Nobody has promised the Mercers a home.

The next morning makes the decision urgent. Jack refuses an unsafe sign-off. His employer fires him; Work records a disputed charge, Cash holds joint wages, Food rejects payment and Home applies the staff-flat deadline. Sarah still has her job. She decides to load the van and ask Bea in person.

Travel through Richmond, Truckee, Wells, Rawlins, North Platte, Kansas and Ava to the Ozarks. Hunt, fish, trade, scavenge, repair the van and evade Safety’s contracted Collectors. A rumor becomes a photograph, then an authenticated reply. Restore Glenn’s pump or carry the Pruitts’ filters to fulfill the entry agreement.

Version 0.8.6 adds 25 new illustrations, distinct evidence scenes, geographic road and fishing environments, local weather/light motion, and a saved record preventing narrative photographs from automatically replaying. Fishing keeps the caught fish in its net. Scavenging uses fixed framing and approaching searchlights. The van retains speed-linked wheels and rear exhaust.

Sound effects now include the vehicle, water, tools, fishing, hunting, paper, keys and Safety. Use **SFX** in the header; Pause contains separate effects and environment levels. The opening has an effects-only track. Music will be supplied separately by the creator. Material sounds use licensed [Kenney CC0 assets](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds); additional effects are designed procedurally.

The nine apps are ID, Cash, Home, Food, Work, Health, Power, Travel and Safety. The approved Human Nature identity and existing brand kit remain. Signals End prohibits phones, computers and digital products, including council equipment. Electricity, mechanical machines and analog radio remain. Entry requires a voluntary choice about the charter. Work ends, a machine stays fixed, and an evening belongs to the family.

Touch is primary; controller and keyboard remain supported. The opening can be skipped and remembers viewing history. Reduced motion freezes scene overlays. Existing v0.7 journeys retain their resources and progress. The [previous demo](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/v06/) keeps its own save.

This is a browser concept playtest, not a native Apple release. Automated desktop/phone browser and simulation checks are distinct from human playtesting or physical-device validation. Illustrations are 1672 × 941; local motion does not imply articulated character animation or voiced performances.
