San Francisco, 2041. Nine technology corporations control the services people need. Cameras watch streets and homes. Rent, food, taxes and mandatory charges rise faster than wages; company contracts barely cover life. Jack repairs machines the service companies want replaced. Sarah helps people their systems declare ineligible.

Bea smuggles in an analog radio. After midnight, Ben hears a voice speaking about Signals End. The family leaves with a handwritten clue, an old van and no promise of safety. The Collectors enforce household debt and relocation orders; the radio does not magically reveal their location.

Follow contacts across the Sierra and the Plains toward an analog refuge in the Missouri-Arkansas Ozarks. Fish, salvage, repair, trade and evade. A witness and a private family reply gradually turn the rumor into an agreement. Repair a community pump or deliver filters to earn a way through. Beyond the Line: no phones, computers or digital products, even for the council. Electricity, mechanical engines and analog radios remain. The reward is control over ordinary life and time with family.
