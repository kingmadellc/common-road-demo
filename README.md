# Signals End / King Made

[Play the demo](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/?v=0.7.6) · [Watch the opening](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/?v=0.7.6&film=1) · [World and brand kit](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/brand/?v=2&origin=1) · [Previous demo and save](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/v06/)

San Francisco, 2041. Nine technology corporations control everyday services. Cameras watch streets and homes. Rent, food, taxes and mandatory charges rise faster than wages; company contracts barely cover life.

Jack keeps checking work after his shift. Sarah repeats herself. Annie waits with a drawing. He wants a simpler life, but neither parent believes one is still possible. Ben tries the dial on a radio Bea smuggled among repair parts. The midnight voice mentions Signals End. Jack wants to find it. Sarah is angry, curious and unwilling to gamble the children on a stranger’s promise. They argue for three nights, write questions and agree on a first step: go across the Bay and ask Bea.

Follow contacts across the Sierra and the Plains toward an analog refuge in the Missouri-Arkansas Ozarks. Fish, salvage, repair, trade and evade. A witness and a private family reply gradually turn the rumor into an agreement. Repair a community pump or deliver filters to earn a way through. Beyond the Line: no phones, computers or digital products, including council equipment. Electricity, mechanical engines and analog radios remain. The reward is time and authority over ordinary life. The Collectors enforce debt and relocation orders; receiving a broadcast does not reveal the family’s location.

The opening now states the dependency directly: “Nine companies run the city. Without their services, you can’t live.”

Version 0.7.6 carries the grounded family motivation through the opening, biographies, packing, journal, Bea’s introduction and road callbacks. Existing journeys keep resources, progress and discovered evidence. The third illustrated film runs 116 seconds, with 123 on-screen words, a new apartment illustration, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next captions and original procedural sound. Previous viewers keep their skip preference. Replay plays the new cut. Character animation and voiced performances are not included.

The nine fictional companies remain Index, Forma, Plenty, Halo, Span, Loop, Vita, Core and Civic. Brand kit edition 02 retains the approved Human Nature identity and includes a 15-page guide, institutional profiles, vector logos, licensed fonts, seven source-resolution art views, editable campaign layouts and a silent title reveal. Source illustrations are 1672 x 941; larger layout exports do not add image detail.
