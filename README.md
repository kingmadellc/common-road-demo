# Signals End · playable concept demo

[Play Signals End](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/?v=0.7.2) · [Brand kit](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/brand/?v=3) · [Previous demo and save](https://kingmadellc.github.io/common-road-demo/v06/)

San Francisco, 2041. Jack and Sarah supervise the AI agents that replaced their old jobs. Cameras score their attention; rent, food and taxes consume the pay. Then Ben finds a smuggled analog radio and hears a cryptic message about Signals End. The family follows that uncertain lead east.

Version 0.7.2 weaves this origin through packing, the family, road encounters, repairs, meals, rest and arrival. Bea’s freight contacts relay Sarah’s private question by analog radio. A North Platte courier records the reply. June’s photograph corroborates the rumor at Truckee. Frank’s authenticated reply confirms a reservation at North Platte. Journey and journal summaries reflect what the family actually knows. Existing v0.7 saves keep their supplies, time and progress; earlier-origin journal entries are labeled as an earlier story draft.

Cross the Sierra, Nevada, Utah, Wyoming, Nebraska and Kansas toward a fictional analog refuge in the Missouri–Arkansas Ozarks. Fish, salvage, repair, evade workforce-recovery squads, and earn a witness or deliver ceramic filters. One roadside terminal offers $60 for another orchestration shift, at the cost of time, rest and a recorded plate. The reward is an analog life: machines you can repair and switch off, a home independent of employment, and an evening with your family.

The current opening remains the earlier 96-second illustrated motion-graphics film. Its revised 92-second script is prepared; the new apartment and nighttime radio scenes have not yet been produced. First visits start muted, Skip is available, viewing is remembered, and Replay opening remains available. Reduced-motion and data-saver preferences bypass autoplay.

Touch first, with controller and keyboard support. New v0.7 saves remain separate from v0.6. This is a browser playtest, not a native Apple release or evidence of human-tested fun.

Validation covers both complete admission paths, 128 simulation tests, touch play, staged story knowledge, paid and declined orchestration choices, and save migration. Build provenance is in build-info.json. The full source is private; this repository contains the public playable payload.
