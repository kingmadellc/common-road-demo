THE IDEA

Human. Stubborn.
Still going.

The rounded forms carry curiosity and a little mischief. Dark landscapes, worn machinery and candid faces carry the stakes. The identity leaves room for both.

04 / ART & VOICE

They’re on an adventure.
They aren’t in an advertisement.

Find a moment. One or two people, imperfect framing, different eye lines. Nervousness, skepticism and wonder belong beside sadness.

Make the machine credible. Worn paint, comprehensible controls, a tool doing a specific job. Keep hands and props simple.

Let the joke come from them. Dry, brief, earned. The family gets to be funny. Their hardship is never the punchline.
